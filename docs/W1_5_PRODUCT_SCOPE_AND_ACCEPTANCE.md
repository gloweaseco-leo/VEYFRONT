## Standard ¥9,800 inclusions (bounded)

1. One client-confirmed target market, buyer task and product/business focus; one agreed page map and editorial outline.
2. Up to 6 distinct designed business pages/templates, brand adaptation with existing approved assets, desktop/tablet/mobile; no original logo, 3D scene or paid photography.
3. Up to 15 templated product records using customer-supplied source material; one key application/solution page; one verified case **or** evidence module where actual proof exists. Never fabricate certifications, specs, testimonials or performance.
4. English + simplified Chinese versions of the same agreed structure. Client approves professional terminology and all substantive product/company claims. No separate bilingual market-research projects or specialist translation.
5. SEO-Native engineering: page-intent outline, meaningful HTML headings, differentiated titles/descriptions, indexable production pages (Preview noindex), accurate canonical/robots/sitemap, internal links, relevant image descriptions, appropriate schema where facts exist, and separate language URLs with reciprocal hreflang. Search Console verification requires customer credentials/permission. Not a guarantee of indexing or ranking.
6. Product-related inquiry form with **server-side accepted/persisted receipt, tested notification and failed-state handling**. WhatsApp and mailto are auxiliary paths: a click alone is not an accepted lead. Contact data protection and customer operator responsibilities require authorization.
7. One analytics integration and documented visit / contact-intent / *actually received* inquiry event distinctions. Test event behavior without personal payload leakage.
8. Two consolidated revision rounds, one deployment assist, rebuildable source/assets/dependency manifest and operating notes; 30-day in-scope delivery defect remedy. Third-party domain, hosting, API fees are itemized, not silently included.

No cross-locale SEO promise unless actual independent URLs + hreflang can be proven on the client deliverable. No customer case is published until evidence and consent exist.
